Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

We store only what is needed for gameplay: IDs, scores/stats, and server settings. We do not sell user data.

1) Data We Store

FieldPurpose
userIdAssociate gameplay scores/stats to a Discord user.
guildIdScope leaderboard + settings to a server.
scorePoints from correct role guesses.
correctGuessesCount of correct guesses.
wrongGuessesCount of wrong guesses.
gameChannelIdConfigured channel restriction per guild.
enabledModesRole pools enabled for that guild (EHR/TOHO).

2) What We Do Not Store

  • We do not store message history beyond command processing.
  • We do not store email addresses, IP addresses, or passwords.
  • We do not sell or rent user data.

3) Storage

Data is stored in MongoDB for persistence. If MongoDB is unavailable, the bot may use in-memory fallback for runtime behavior.

4) Retention

Scores and settings are retained while the service runs. You may request deletion by contacting the project maintainer with your Discord ID and server ID.

5) Third Parties

The bot operates on Discord and may use MongoDB infrastructure. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's own Terms and Privacy Policy.

6) Contact

For questions, open an post on the server: Discord Server.
